## Overall Impression

The underlying substance is unusually strong for a junior portfolio — real numbers, a real named tool, defensible technical narratives. The presentation layer mostly serves that substance well (restrained palette, working progressive disclosure, real keyboard/escape handling in overlays), but three things actively undercut it: a fully-built fourth case study that no visitor can ever reach, a final-CTA headline that inverts the site's own stated priority (freelance framing ahead of job-search framing), and a false-affordance pattern on the flagship project cards that costs a click exactly where the candidate is trying to prove competence. None of these are visual-taste problems — they're wiring and message-ordering bugs sitting on top of good work.

## What's Working

1. **The four-phase "Investigar → Preparar → Analizar → Comunicar" structure does real positioning work.** Each phase pairs a concrete deliverable with confident, specific copy — this demonstrates the "Power BI + UX judgment + storytelling" positioning as a process, not an adjective.
2. **Project narratives use real, quantified problem→solution→result structure**, not generic "built a dashboard" claims — the load-bearing numbers are the actual proof of competence.
3. **Accessibility mechanics exceed baseline in specific, verifiable ways**: real `aria-expanded` + keyboard handling on the accordion (not just visual toggling), Escape/click-outside on the lightbox, `prefers-reduced-motion` correctly zeroes animation, descriptive (not filler) alt text throughout.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] A fully-written fourth project case study is unreachable by any visitor.**
Why it matters: `idx-overlay-3` ("Kosta Cálida — Reporting para red de tiendas") is a complete case study — disconnected `T_DISC` tables, dual fact tables, dynamic TopN parameters, three real images, a fully working lightbox (`openIndexOverlay(3)`/`closeIndexOverlay(3)` and nav arrows all function) — but no element on the visible page calls `openIndexOverlay(3)`. Only indices 0, 1, 2 are wired to the three visible cards. Real proof-of-work sits in the DOM, costing nothing to show, and shows to no one.
Fix: Add a fourth `proj-card` (or fold into the existing row) that calls `openIndexOverlay(3)`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] The final CTA headline leads with freelance framing, inverting the site's own stated priority.**
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md states explicitly that the primary signal is "busco incorporarme como Data Analyst" and freelance must not compete in visual weight with that. Yet the largest, boldest text in the final decision-critical section is "¿Tienes un proyecto? Hablemos." — pure client/freelance framing — while the job-search framing only appears in 15.5px regular-weight subtext beneath it. A recruiter skimming the close sees "do you have a project?" before anything that says this candidate is job-hunting.
Fix: Flip the framing so the H2 addresses hiring/role-seeking first, with freelance as the secondary line.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] False affordance on all three visible project cards, confirmed via live computed styles.**
Why it matters: `getComputedStyle(.proj-card).cursor === 'pointer'` plus a whole-card `:active { transform: scale(.98) }` press animation — but `card.onclick === null`. Only the small "Ver →" text button at the bottom-right actually responds. The image and title — the largest, most "clickable-looking" elements, and the natural first click for someone evaluating evidence of skill — are dead. On touch devices there's no hover cue to warn users beforehand.
Fix: Make the whole card clickable with a single handler calling the same `openIndexOverlay`, or remove the pointer cursor/press animation from the non-interactive area.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Multiple WCAG AA contrast failures, confirmed against live computed/rendered colors.**
Why it matters: shared.css already defines AA-compliant muted tokens (`--muted-light` 5.74:1, `--mutedB` 4.88:1, documented inline) but several real instances don't use them: hero social-proof line (3.04:1), "— abre cada fase" hint (2.32:1), star-project credential line (2.85:1), phase numbers 01–04 (3.44:1), and the flagship Casa Origen project's own CTA button "Ver caso completo →" (white on `--purple`, 3.44:1) — all fail the 4.5:1 AA minimum for normal text. The live browser scan additionally flagged several purple-on-white/lavender instances (3.0–3.4:1, repeated). One severe outlier the scan reported as ~1.0:1 on `<a class="btn btn-outline">Ver todos los proyectos →</a>` (line 1844) was investigated and traced to a **detector false positive**: the detector reads the declared `background-color` of `.btn::before` (a hover-swipe fill pseudo-element that sits `translateY(101%)` off-screen at rest) as if it were the button's real background, and compares it against the button's own text color. At rest the button's real background is transparent — genuinely high contrast, no fix needed there.
Fix: Swap the five listed elements to `--muted-light`/`--mutedB`; darken `--purple` specifically for the star-box CTA background, or switch its text to `--ink`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Decision overload at the single most important moment on the page.**
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md's own success definition names only two actions that matter ("Envíame un mensaje" / "Agendar llamada"), but the final CTA row presents four buttons at similar visual weight — one filled and three visually-equal outline buttons ("Agendar llamada", "Descargar CV", "LinkedIn"). Every additional co-equal choice at a decision point measurably reduces the odds of any single action being taken.
Fix: Demote "Descargar CV" and "LinkedIn" to plain text links below the two primary buttons.
Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

## Minor Observations

- The nav "Contacto" button's accessible name is a concatenated run-on — `textContent` reads `"Contacto¿Hablamos?"` with no space, because neither hover-swap span is `aria-hidden`. A screen reader announces both words squashed together.
- "ES · EN" sits as a trust pill in the final CTA beside "MADRID, ESPAÑA" — but there is no language-switch link, no `hreflang`, and no reference to `/en/` anywhere on this page, so if read as a site feature it's currently undiscoverable from the homepage.
- "Seis problemas reales" is followed by two visually unrelated card systems (3 white bordered cards, then a divider, then 3 black `ml-grid` cards) — the "seis" framing reads as two separate three-item groups rather than one coherent six.
- `side-tab` pattern confirmed present: the "Cómo trabajo" accordion's open-state indicator is a 6px lime accent stripe growing in from the left — functional (marks the open phase) but a recognizably generic "AI-UI" visual tell, consistent with the same pattern the 13 Aug site-wide critique flagged elsewhere.
- Two `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` hits (hero `.dashboard-frame`, project lightbox `.pc-overlay-inner`) were reviewed in context and judged purposeful — a floating browser-window mockup and a genuine full-screen modal both reasonably want a soft, large-radius elevation shadow. Flagged mechanically, not a real defect.
- 4 headings (`h3` in the featured-project deep-dive) show inconsistent spacing (0px above vs 14px below), reading as visually bound to the block above rather than introducing their own section.
